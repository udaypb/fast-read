Read Better

Reading better is not only about reading faster. ReadFast.live helps you practice focused pacing so you can keep attention on the words and adjust speed when comprehension changes.

A Simple Reading Practice

  1. Add a short article, note, or PDF.
  2. Start at a comfortable WPM.
  3. Pause or slow down for dense sections.
  4. Increase speed for familiar material.
  5. Review anything important after the first pass.

Why Pacing Helps

A paced reader can reduce wandering attention by giving your eyes a consistent target. ReadFast.live combines pacing with controls for speed, word grouping, and document input.
