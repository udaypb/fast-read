Free Speed Reader Online

ReadFast.live is a free speed reader for focused reading in the browser. Paste text, import a PDF, set words per minute, and start reading without installing a separate app.

Free Reading Features

  • Free online speed reader for pasted text.
  • PDF import for documents and study material.
  • Adjustable WPM controls for faster or slower reading.
  • Word grouping for single-word or multi-word pacing.
  • Focused full-screen reader UI.

When to Use It

Use ReadFast.live when you want a quick reading pass through notes, articles, PDFs, or drafts and want to control the speed directly.
