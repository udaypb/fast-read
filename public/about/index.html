About ReadFast.live

ReadFast.live is a free speed reader, also discoverable by people searching for ReadFast or FastRead, built for anyone who wants a focused way to move through text, notes, articles, and PDF documents in the browser.

What ReadFast.live Does

The app lets readers paste text, import PDFs, adjust words per minute, change word grouping, and read with a distraction-free interface designed to keep attention centered.

Why the Name Matters

The official site is ReadFast.live. People may type ReadFast, Read Fast, or FastRead when looking for the speed reader, but the live browser app is published at www.readfast.live.

Who It Is For

ReadFast.live is useful for students, researchers, professionals, and anyone looking for a simple online speed reader without installing a separate desktop app.

Reading Features

ReadFast.live supports pasted text, PDF text extraction, adjustable WPM, one-word and grouped-word pacing, saved reading sessions in the browser, and a full-screen reading interface.
