Reading Tool Online

ReadFast.live is a reading tool online for focused reading sessions. It helps you move through text or PDFs with adjustable pacing instead of scanning a long page manually.

What the Tool Does

  • Turns pasted text into a paced reading session.
  • Extracts readable text from PDF documents.
  • Lets you control words per minute.
  • Supports single-word and grouped-word reading.
  • Keeps reading controls in a focused browser interface.

Best For

Use it for notes, articles, essays, documentation, study material, and drafts when you want a controlled reading pass before deeper review.
