Speed Reading Tool

ReadFast.live is a speed reading tool for readers who want direct control over reading pace. Use it with pasted text or PDFs and adjust words per minute as you read.

Controls

  • Words-per-minute speed adjustment.
  • Single-word and grouped-word pacing.
  • Play, pause, rewind, and forward controls.
  • PDF and pasted text input.

Focused Reading Workflow

The tool is designed for a focused reading pass: add material, start at a comfortable speed, then adjust the pace based on comprehension.
