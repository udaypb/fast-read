Read Fast Online

ReadFast.live is a free browser tool for people who want to read fast with pasted text, PDF documents, adjustable WPM, and a focused reading screen.

How ReadFast.live Helps You Read Fast

  • Paste text and start a paced reading session.
  • Import a PDF and extract readable text in the browser.
  • Set words per minute to match your current reading speed.
  • Use grouped words or single-word pacing for focus.
  • Pause, rewind, and adjust speed as comprehension changes.

Start With a Comfortable Pace

Reading fast is most useful when comprehension stays intact. Start at a comfortable WPM, increase gradually, and slow down when the material is technical or unfamiliar.
