Reading Tools Online

ReadFast.live is one option for people comparing reading tools online. It focuses on fast setup, adjustable pacing, PDF support, and a clean reading surface.

What to Look For

  • Easy text input from paste or document upload.
  • Speed controls that can be changed while reading.
  • PDF support for school, work, and research documents.
  • A focused interface without unnecessary navigation.
  • Clear play, pause, rewind, and forward controls.

How ReadFast.live Fits

ReadFast.live is designed for a focused reading pass. Open the app, add text or a PDF, set a comfortable WPM, and adjust the pace as the material gets easier or harder.
