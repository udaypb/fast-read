Improve Reading Speed Online

ReadFast.live gives you an online way to practice reading speed with real material. Use pasted text or PDFs, then adjust words per minute as you build comfort.

Practice Workflow

  • Use short, familiar material first.
  • Choose a WPM that still feels understandable.
  • Increase speed gradually rather than jumping too far.
  • Use grouped words when single-word reading feels choppy.
  • Slow down for technical writing or unfamiliar vocabulary.

Use Real Documents

Practicing with real documents is more useful than only taking a reading test. ReadFast.live supports text and PDFs so you can practice with the material you already need to read.
