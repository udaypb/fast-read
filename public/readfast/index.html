ReadFast.live

ReadFast.live is the official ReadFast speed reader site. It is a free browser app for reading pasted text and PDF documents with adjustable pacing.

Official ReadFast Site

If you are looking for ReadFast, Read Fast, or FastRead, the browser-based speed reader is available at www.readfast.live. The homepage opens directly into the reading experience.

What You Can Do

Use ReadFast.live to paste text, import PDFs, change words per minute, adjust word grouping, and read in a focused full-screen interface.
