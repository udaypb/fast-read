Free Speed Reader Online

ReadFast.live is a free online speed reader for focused reading. Paste text, upload a PDF, set your reading speed, and move through words in a clean browser-based reader for ReadFast and FastRead searches.

Speed Reader Features

  • Paste plain text into an online speed reader.
  • Import PDF documents and extract readable text.
  • Adjust words per minute to match your reading pace.
  • Change word chunk size for single-word or grouped reading.
  • Use focused reading controls in a distraction-free interface.

What Makes ReadFast.live Different

ReadFast.live focuses on getting you into the reader quickly. There is no marketing page to step through first: the homepage opens the reading experience, and the import controls let you add text or a PDF right away.

The reader is designed for adjustable pacing rather than a fixed reading test. Start at a comfortable WPM, increase or decrease the pace, and use grouped words when single-word reading feels too slow.
