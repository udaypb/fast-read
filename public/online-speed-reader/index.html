Online Speed Reader

ReadFast.live is an online speed reader for people who want to read text and PDFs in a focused browser workflow. The reader lets you adjust pacing instead of scrolling through full pages manually.

Browser-Based Reading

Open the reader, add text or a PDF, and control the reading speed with WPM settings. The app runs in the browser and keeps reading controls close to the text.

Text and PDF Input

ReadFast.live supports pasted text and PDF import, making it useful for notes, articles, study material, and longer documents.
